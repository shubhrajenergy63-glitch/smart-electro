SMART ELECTRO - MARKETPLACE BUILD

Start on Windows:
1. Open CMD in this folder.
2. npm install
3. npm start
4. Open http://localhost:3000


Included workflows:
- Customer and professional Aadhaar KYC fields (number + front/back photos + OTP gate).
- Customer profile photo upload after login; no licence field for customers.
- Customer problem-photo upload for requirements; private to Admin and relevant professional.
- Admin-only KYC/document visibility.
- Manual and Auto completion-photo approval. Switching to Auto also processes eligible pending completion photos.
- Payment blocked until approved completion and an Admin-set final amount exists.
- Payment methods: UPI, Cash, Card with method-specific reference fields.
- Payment Completed only after payment; Close only after payment and no open complaint.
- Job chat between customer and assigned professional.
- Invoice generation after payment, printable/savable from browser.
- GPS location and professional location updates.
- Public approved completed-work showcase with likes/comments/saves/share.
- Admin-only company branding/logo.
- Powered by Shubhraj Energy Systems.

Aadhaar note:
The local OTP endpoint is DEMO/LOCAL only. Real Aadhaar-linked OTP/authentication requires an authorised UIDAI-compliant KYC/authentication provider and production credentials. Do not treat the demo OTP as real Aadhaar verification.
